About ElderSafe AI

An AI-assisted computer vision system designed to help identify possible falls among elderly people through camera-based monitoring.

Capture

02

Analyze

Frames are preprocessed and analyzed using a custom-trained CNN model.

03

Notify

Possible fall events are recorded and made available through the notification system.
